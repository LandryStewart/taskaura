TaskAura started with a simple idea: teams shouldn't need five tools to manage their work. We built one fast workspace that keeps everyone in sync.

Our Story

TaskAura was born out of frustration with bloated project management tools that slow teams down instead of speeding them up. We wanted something lightweight, real-time, and free — a tool that gets out of the way and lets teams focus on the work that matters.

We built TaskAura as a single, fast workspace where tasks, notes, calendars, and team chat live together. No tab-switching, no sync delays, no per-seat pricing that punishes you for growing your team.

What We Believe

⚡
Speed over ceremony
Tools should be fast to open, fast to use, and fast to close. Every millisecond of friction adds up.
🔓
Free means free
Every feature, every team member, $0. We believe productivity tools shouldn't have a paywall.
🔄
Real-time by default
Your team should never wonder if they're looking at stale data. Changes sync instantly, always.
🛡️
Your data, your control
We don't sell your data. We don't use tracking ads. Your workspace data stays yours, exportable at any time.
